## 1\. 经典三列（48 × 36 英寸）

这正是大多数海报展示会都会用的布局，原因很充分：它读起来像一张纸，从左到右排列，所以访客总能知道下一步该往哪里走。中间那一栏是留给你最大的两个人物的，因为当人们停在板前时，第一眼就是在那里。

- 当你的研究有清晰的 IMRaD 故事和两三个主要数据时 **，才会用它** 。
- **章节：** 引言、目标、方法（以工作流图形式，而非段落）、跨两栏结果、讨论、高亮的结论框、参考文献。
- **文件中的字体大小：** 标题 88 点，章节标题 44 点，正文 28 点，说明和参考文献 22 点。

## 2\. A0 肖像，两列（841 × 1189 mm）

A0是大多数欧洲和亚洲会议的默认海报尺寸。它高而窄，所以两列比三列更易读;三柱宽841毫米，每柱过于薄，不适合采用反应方案或表征面板。标题下方的全宽抽象带让浏览者能在一个地方完整了解整个故事。

- 当海报征集写着A0或“纵向，最大宽度841毫米”时 **使用** ，这在国际化学与材料会议上很常见。
- **章节：** 摘要带、引言、综合（方案）、表征（XRD、NMR、SEM 面板）、结果图、对比表、结论、参考文献。
- **文件中的类型大小：** 标题72点，标题36点，正文24点。
- **发电机说明：** A0 的比例为 1：1.41，而生成器提供最接近的竖像比例为 3：4（1：1.33）。当你把生成的设计放在 A0 页面上时，可以预期顶部和底部会有一些额外的空间。

## 3\. 临床研究，肖像（36 × 48 英寸）

临床海报有两样其他领域可以省略的内容：参与者流程图和基线特征表。这种布局让它们都有一个固定的归属，而不是把它们压缩到一个结果列里。结论条带横贯底部，宽度全开，从通道上也能阅读，另外一个区块放置信息披露和资金，这是大多数医学会议的要求。

- 当您在医疗或公共卫生会议上展示试验、队列或质量改进项目时 **，请使用它** 。
- **章节：** 背景、目标、方法（CONSORT风格流程图）、结果（基线表及结果图）、结论、参考文献和披露。
- **提示：** 用专用工具（比如 [CONSORT 图生成器](https://conceptviz.app/tools/consort-diagram-generator) ）制作流程图并粘贴进去，而不是在海报里手绘方框。

## 4\. 广告牌：中心主要发现（48 × 36 英寸）

这种版式由迈克·莫里森在2019年推出的“更佳海报”推广开来。中间一栏以一句通俗语言、标题大小的句子陈述你的主要发现，下面有一个关键数字。其他所有内容（方法、统计数据、额外图表）都被转移到了狭窄的侧栏，供那些停下来继续阅读的人看。当你能用一句话诚实地表达结果时，效果最好。

- 当你的观众走过几十张海报，而你需要他们在五秒内明白你要看点时 **，就要用它**;在心理学、认知科学和社会科学课程中很常见。
- **章节：** 主要发现（中间），关键图表，引言，方法，参与者，额外结果，统计表，参考文献。
- **要小心：** 有些评委仍然期望有一个明显的“方法”部分。这个模板会把一个留在左栏，这样海报仍然能通过传统的审核。

## 5\. 以结果为先的网格（48 × 36 英寸）

计算机科学和工程海报通常由已经了解背景、想了解方法和数据的人阅读。该网格以一行关键贡献开头，将架构或系统图宽度的三分之二提供给出，并将基准表和消融图表并排展示，便于一目了然地比较。

- 当你在机器学习、机器人或系统类场地展示时 **，** 海报作为论文的配套，可以使用它。
- **章节：** 主要贡献：方法（架构图）、问题、数据集、基准测试（表格）、消融（图表）、局限性与链接（论文、代码、二维码）。
- **提示：** 如果你的场地用的是36×24英寸或A1尺寸的板子，同样的结构也可以;在缩小表格之前，先将架构示意图缩小到宽度的一半。

## 6\. A1实地研究（594×841毫米）

实地考察从一个地点开始，所以这个布局是从一张地图开始的。研究区域地图位于左上角，抽样细节位于其旁边，中间则有两张相等的结果图表，方便你展示对比和趋势。最底层的范围是留给暗示性内容，这正是保护和政策读者想要阅读的内容。

- 当你在小型会议或部门日展示生态、地理或环境相关工作时 **，使用** A1板。
- **章节：** 研究区域（地图）、导言、采样、两张结果图表、保护意义、参考文献。
- **文件中的字体大小：** 标题60点，标题30点，正文22点，因为A1海报是从近距离阅读的。

## 7\. 紧凑研讨会（36×24英寸）

Undergraduate research days and campus symposiums often use smaller 36 × 24 inch boards or print on a 24-inch roll. With less space, the template commits to one big results figure in the center and keeps the text to a research question, short methods, a conclusion, and next steps. It is also a good first poster for anyone who has never made one.

- **Use it when** your board is 36 × 24 in or your print shop uses 24-inch paper.
- **Sections:** Research question, Methods, Results (one main figure), Conclusion, Next steps, References.
- **Generator note:** 36 × 24 is a 3:2 ratio. The generator's closest landscape option is 4:3, so expect a slightly taller design to crop or pad.

## How to edit the PowerPoint template

1. **Check the page size first.** In PowerPoint, open Design > Slide Size > Custom Slide Size. It should already match the template (for example 48 × 36 in, or 33.11 × 46.81 in for A0). Confirm it against your conference instructions before adding anything.
2. **Replace the title and author line.** Keep the title to two lines. If it wraps to three, shorten it rather than shrinking the font.
3. **Drop in figures.** Click a dashed figure box, note its size, delete it, and use Insert > Pictures to place your figure in the same spot. Export figures from R, Python, Prism, or Excel as PNG at 300 ppi at the size they will print, or as vector PDF or SVG where your software allows. For schematic icons, a free library or a paid tool such as Mind the Graph works; our [Mind the Graph review](https://conceptviz.app/blog/mind-the-graph-review-pricing-alternatives) explains which of its plans allow publication.
4. **Paste text as plain text.** Use Paste Special > Unformatted Text so your manuscript's fonts and spacing do not come along. Aim for short bullet points; most posters work best under about 800 words in total.
5. **Recolor in one step.** Each template uses one accent color for the header and section bars. Select them together and change the fill to your institution's color.
6. **Export to PDF at full size.** Use File > Export > PDF (or Save As PDF) and check that the PDF page size matches the poster size. Most print shops want a PDF, not a PPTX.

## Poster size and print checklist

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Board size | Use the exact size in the call for posters. Common: 48 × 36 in, 36 × 48 in, A0 (841 × 1189 mm), A1 (594 × 841 mm) |
| PowerPoint limit | PowerPoint slides max out at 56 in per side. For larger boards, design at half scale and ask the printer to print at 200% |
| Image resolution | 150 ppi at final size is the usual minimum; 300 ppi is safer for photos and micrographs |
| Title | 72 to 100 pt so it is readable from about 3 m (10 ft) |
| Body text | 24 to 32 pt on a 48-inch poster; 22 pt is the floor on A1 |
| Margins | Keep text at least 1 in (25 mm) from the edge; trimming can cut closer than you expect |
| Colors | Check contrast and avoid red-green pairs; see our [scientific color palette guide](https://conceptviz.app/blog/scientific-color-palette-for-research-papers-and-posters) |
| File | PDF with fonts embedded, at the exact print size |

## Common template mistakes

- **Designing on a 16:9 slide and scaling up.** The ratio will not match the board, and every font size will be wrong. Start from a file that is already the right size.
- **Filling every box.** A template's boxes are suggestions. Delete a section rather than padding it.
- **Keeping placeholder figures.** Generated or stock figures are for layout only. Every figure on a scientific poster should come from your own data or be credited.
- **Leaving the template color scheme when your institution has one.** Many universities require their brand colors or logo placement for conference posters; check before you print.
- **Not proofreading at 100% zoom.** Typos that are invisible on a laptop screen are obvious on a 4-foot print.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these scientific poster templates free?

Yes. All seven PowerPoint templates are free to download and use for academic and commercial posters. No sign-up is needed to download them. Opening a template in the poster generator is also free to try.

### Where can I get a free scientific poster template for PowerPoint?

On this page. The seven layouts are free.pptx downloads with no sign-up. They use only standard shapes, text boxes, and one native table, so they also open in Keynote and import into Google Slides (Google Slides may rescale the page; check File > Page setup).

### What size should a research poster template be?

Use the size in your conference instructions. In the US the most common sizes are 48 x 36 inches (landscape) and 36 x 48 inches (portrait). Many international meetings use A0 (841 x 1189 mm), and smaller events use A1 (594 x 841 mm) or 36 x 24 inches.

### Can I use these templates in Google Slides or Keynote?

Yes. The files use only standard shapes, text boxes, and a native table, so they open in Keynote and import into Google Slides. Google Slides may change the page size on import, so check File > Page setup and set the custom size again if needed.

### What does the 'Open in the poster generator' button do?

It opens the ConceptViz scientific poster generator with that template's layout already written as a prompt and the closest aspect ratio selected. Replace the example study at the end of the prompt with your own, then generate a designed draft of that layout.

### Is the AI-generated poster ready to print?

Treat it as a design draft. The generator outputs a 2K image, which is too low in resolution for a sharp 48-inch print, and any data or references the model writes are placeholders. Rebuild the final version in the PowerPoint template with your real figures, or use the image directly for a digital poster screen.

### What resolution should poster figures be?

Aim for 300 pixels per inch at the size the figure will print, and no less than 150 ppi. A figure that will be 10 inches wide on the poster should be about 3,000 pixels wide. Vector formats such as PDF or SVG avoid the problem for charts.

### How many words should a scientific poster have?

Most poster guides recommend staying under about 800 words. Visitors spend only a few minutes at each poster, so use figures to carry the results and keep text to short bullet points.

### Which template is best for a first conference poster?

The compact symposium template (36 x 24 in) or the classic three-column template (48 x 36 in). Both follow a simple left-to-right reading order and need only two or three figures.
